Privacy Policy

Effective Date: July 6, 2026
Service Name: RainMail (雨天信箱)
Service Address: https://rm.techccy.com

RainMail (雨天信箱) is an anonymous emotional interaction platform operated by TechCCY. We deeply understand the importance of privacy. This policy explains how we collect, use, store, and protect your information. In addition to anonymous posting, this service also offers optional registered accounts, emotional letter delivery, and reply notifications. Please read carefully; using this service indicates your agreement to this policy.

1. Information We Collect

To ensure normal operation and security of the service, we collect the following information:

1.1 Content You Submit Actively

  • Account Information (Registered Users): Email address (for login and notifications), username (optional; defaults to the email prefix if omitted), password (stored only as a one-way hash; we do not store plaintext passwords), and the city derived from your IP via reverse lookup.
  • Message Content: Text messages you deliver on sunny days (up to 500 characters), stored in an overseas server database and displayed to other users when it rains in the target city. No fixed retention period.
  • Emotional Letters: Delivery type (public / specified recipient / random verified user), recipient email (optional), and delivery options.
  • Reply Content: Text replies you write to others (up to 500 characters).
  • Contact Email (Optional): An email you may optionally provide when posting anonymously to receive reply notifications.

1.2 Automatically Collected Technical Information

Information Type Collection Method Usage Purpose Retention Period
IP Address Server automatic logging Prevent abuse, security auditing, city reverse lookup Automatically deleted after 30 days
User-Agent Browser automatic sending Optimize service compatibility Deleted after session ends
Access Time/Page Server logs Troubleshooting Automatically deleted after 30 days

1.3 Information Collected or Processed by Third-Party Services

  • QWeather API: Only transmits city ID (e.g., 101280101, Guangzhou) to obtain weather status, no personal user information is transmitted.
  • ip-api.com: Resolves your IP address into a city name (for weather display and localization). This is a plaintext HTTP request that transmits your IP. See ip-api.com docs.
  • AI Content Moderation Service: The full text of the messages and replies you submit is sent to a third-party AI service we configure for content safety review. This service is located overseas.
  • SMTP Email Service: Sends email verification, letter delivery, and reply notification emails via a third-party SMTP service. Email content includes the recipient email and a message digest.

2. Information We Do Not Collect

  • No real name, phone number, or national ID number required (only an email address is needed to register an account)
  • No cross-site behavior tracking; no commercial user profiling
  • No collection of device unique identifiers (such as IMEI, MAC address)
  • No use of data for advertising or commercial marketing; no third-party analytics or statistics scripts integrated
⚠️ Important Compliance Notice

Due to the domain name of this service not completing ICP filing, and using overseas dynamic domain deployment, this service cannot actively provide access services to users in mainland China. If you are located in mainland China, please exit this service voluntarily. Any legal risks arising from violation of this regulation when using this service shall be borne by the user.

3. Purpose of Information Use

We only use collected information for the following purposes:

  1. Provide core service: Display anonymous messages during rainy days, and provide letter delivery and reply features
  2. Account identification and session maintenance: login authentication and keeping you logged in
  3. Email notifications: email verification, letter delivery notices, and reply reminders
  4. Localized display: resolving your city from your IP to provide the corresponding weather and localized content
  5. AI-assisted content safety moderation: identifying policy-violating content in messages and replies
  6. Ensure service security: identify and block abusive behaviors (such as spam, attacks, brute-force attempts)
  7. Service optimization: adjust page compatibility based on device information
  8. Legal compliance: cooperate with judicial authorities to legally retrieve data (such as receiving valid legal documents)

4. Information Sharing and Disclosure

We will not sell, rent, or trade any of your information. Information may only be disclosed under the following circumstances:

  1. Legal requirements: According to applicable laws, regulations, judicial or administrative procedures
  2. Security protection: To prevent fraud, abuse or security incidents, limited information may be provided to necessary third parties
  3. Service necessity: Transmit necessary data to the following third-party service providers:
    • Cloudflare (bot verification)
    • QWeather (weather data query)
    • ip-api.com (IP geolocation)
    • Third-party AI content moderation service (reviewing message/reply text)
    • Third-party SMTP email service (sending notification emails)
  4. Business transfer: In case of merger, acquisition or asset sale, user information may be part of the transferred assets

5. Data Security Measures

We take the following measures to protect your data:

  • Use parameterized queries (Drizzle ORM) to prevent SQL injection attacks
  • XSS filtering and SQL injection signature detection for user input
  • Passwords are stored as one-way scrypt hashes; no plaintext is kept
  • Session cookies are set with HttpOnly and SameSite attributes; Secure is optionally enabled in production
  • Write operations are protected by CSRF tokens
  • Login failure lockout on both email and IP dimensions to defend against brute force
  • Honeypot fields and behavioral verification (page dwell time, input interactions) to defend against automated bots
  • Periodic cleanup of unverified accounts (deleted if the email is not verified within 60 minutes of registration)
  • Servers deployed in a firewall-protected network environment with security response headers (CSP, etc.)

6. Data Breach Response

We will take the following measures to respond to data breach incidents:

  • Emergency Plan: We have developed a data breach response plan and conduct regular drills
  • Notification Obligation: In the event of a data breach that may affect your rights and interests, we will:
    • Assess the impact within 72 hours of becoming aware
    • If it may pose a risk to you, notify via website announcement or email
    • Notification includes nature of breach, approximate scope, and recommended remedial measures

7. Data Storage and Cross-Border Transfer

  • All data stored on overseas servers
  • Due to service deployment overseas, your information may be transmitted to locations outside your country/region for processing
  • Message and reply text is transmitted to an overseas AI moderation service endpoint during the content review phase
  • Your IP address is queried via ip-api.com for geolocation
  • We will take reasonable measures to ensure cross-border data transmission complies with relevant legal requirements

11. Cookie and Tracking Technologies

This service uses a cookie named session to maintain your login session and basic functionality. This cookie:

  • is protected by HttpOnly and SameSite attributes; Secure is optionally enabled in production;
  • has a validity period of 24 hours and is used only for identification and session maintenance, not for behavior tracking or advertising;
  • is signed with an HMAC by the server and does not store plaintext sensitive information.

This service does not integrate any third-party analytics or statistics scripts such as Google Analytics or Baidu Tongji. If you disable cookies in your browser, features that require session maintenance (such as login) may not work properly; anonymous browsing is unaffected.
