Privacy Policy

Effective Date: May 29, 2026
Service Name: RainMail (雨天信箱)
Service Address: https://dropbox.techccy.dpdns.org

RainMail (雨天信箱) is an anonymous emotional interaction platform operated by TechCCY. We deeply understand the importance of privacy. This policy explains how we collect, use, store, and protect your information. Please read carefully; using this service indicates your agreement to this policy.

1. Information We Collect

Although this service is designed to be anonymous, to ensure normal operation and security of the service, we collect the following information:

1.1 Content You Submit Actively

  • Message Content: Text messages you deliver on sunny days (up to 500 characters)
  • Usage Purpose: Display to other users when it rains in the target city
  • Storage Location: Overseas server database
  • Retention Period: No fixed period

1.2 Automatically Collected Technical Information

Information Type Collection Method Usage Purpose Retention Period
IP Address Server automatic logging Prevent abuse, security auditing Automatically deleted after 30 days
User-Agent Browser automatic sending Optimize service compatibility Deleted after session ends
Access Time/Page Server logs Troubleshooting Automatically deleted after 30 days

1.3 Information Collected by Third-Party Services

  • Cloudflare Turnstile: Used for bot verification to prevent robot abuse. Verification data is processed by Cloudflare, see Cloudflare Privacy Policy
  • QWeather API: Only transmits city ID (101280101, Guangzhou) to obtain weather status, no personal user information is transmitted

2. Information We Do Not Collect

  • No phone numbers, email addresses, names or other personally identifiable information required
  • No tracking of user behavior patterns or creation of user profiles
  • No collection of device unique identifiers (such as IMEI, MAC address)
  • No use of data for advertising or commercial marketing
⚠️ Important Compliance Notice

Due to the domain name of this service not completing ICP filing, and using overseas dynamic domain deployment, this service cannot actively provide access services to users in mainland China. If you are located in mainland China, please exit this service voluntarily. Any legal risks arising from violation of this regulation when using this service shall be borne by the user.

3. Purpose of Information Use

We only use collected information for the following purposes:

  1. Provide core service: Display anonymous messages to users during rainy days
  2. Ensure service security: Identify and block abusive behaviors (such as spam, attacks)
  3. Service optimization: Adjust page compatibility based on device information
  4. Legal compliance: Cooperate with judicial authorities to legally retrieve data (such as receiving valid legal documents)

4. Information Sharing and Disclosure

We will not sell, rent, or trade any of your information. Information may only be disclosed under the following circumstances:

  1. Legal requirements: According to applicable laws, regulations, judicial or administrative procedures
  2. Security protection: To prevent fraud, abuse or security incidents, limited information may be provided to necessary third parties
  3. Service necessity: Transmit necessary data to the following third-party service providers:
    • Cloudflare (bot verification)
    • QWeather (weather data query)
  4. Business transfer: In case of merger, acquisition or asset sale, user information may be part of the transferred assets

5. Data Security Measures

We take the following measures to protect your data:

  • Use parameterized queries to prevent SQL injection attacks
  • XSS filtering for user input
  • Servers deployed in firewall-protected network environment
  • Regular automatic cleanup of expired data (messages 180 days, logs 30 days)
  • Sensitive operations (such as content submission) enable Cloudflare Turnstile bot verification

6. Data Breach Response

We will take the following measures to respond to data breach incidents:

  • Emergency Plan: We have developed a data breach response plan and conduct regular drills
  • Notification Obligation: In the event of a data breach that may affect your rights and interests, we will:
    • Assess the impact within 72 hours of becoming aware
    • If it may pose a risk to you, notify via website announcement or email
    • Notification includes nature of breach, approximate scope, and recommended remedial measures
